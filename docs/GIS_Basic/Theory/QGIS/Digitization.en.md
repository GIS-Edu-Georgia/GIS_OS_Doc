# The Georgian term: დიგიტალიზაცია vs დიგიტიზაცია

This page is about Georgian terminology. In English the process is called **digitization** (or digitizing).

✅ **Correct in Georgian:** _დიგიტალიზაცია_ (digitalizatsia)  
❌ **Incorrect in Georgian:** _დიგიტიზაცია_ (digitizatsia)

---

## 🧾 Definition

**Digitization** means converting physical or analog data into a digital format.

📌 For example:
> Scanning a paper document and saving it as a PDF file.

---

## 🧠 Why not „დიგიტიზაცია“?

- The word **„დიგიტიზაცია“** is terminologically incorrect in Georgian.
- The correctly adopted Georgian form of the English *digitization* is **დიგიტალიზაცია**.
- This term is established in Georgian technical and academic vocabulary.

---

## 📚 Conclusion

In Georgian, use only **დიგიტალიზაცია** ✅  
_It is the correct, standard form in the Georgian language._
